INTERVENTION

Intervention is one of our first ever titles that actually went into testing, unless you count A CALL TO ARMS that didn't make it into the testing stage, this is the game we have been working on for the longest time. Featuring fabulous graphics, a wide variety of weapons, beginner-friendly Map Creation, 6 gamemodes to choose from, it rivals big shooters like COD and CS2. We have been working on Intervention since 2022, and testing has been out since 2024, grab your keys boys! This game also features hosting servers, you can download a 20GB file to host a server on the server browser, you must Login using RINU ID however. LAN also should work with little to zero ping on an average home network but we mostly stay on Peer 2 Peer servers for Matchmaking. Did I mention matchmaking? Yes, this game features matchmaking, our peak was 120 testers playing at once and now we average about 80, every person counts. Why haven't you heard of this game? We do not hire big youtubers, only friends of friends, but this game is staying key-only till 2029, yes, 3 years from when I am writing this. We hope to see you out there on the battlefield, capturing hostages or guarding hostages. - Article written by Jack.

A CALL TO ARMS: A Cancelled Masterpiece

"The first game we ever worked on, but it was too ambitious, we always wanted to come back to it but it was more of a War Thunder Clone" - says Brandon "finebrandy" Steele This game went pretty far into production but it was simply too ambitious at the time It was meant to be a tank game, with air-battles added into it, we were trying to be a better war-thunder. The problem with it was we just could not figure out the vehicle physics. "Which is better? A shitty tank you can't control whatsoever and doesn't adapt to it's terrain or one that is quite the opposite?" says Bella Sundell. - Article written by Johnathan "vluxo" Xhao
